Starting point: the perception of a slight volume imbalance between the
left and right channels on a portable music player with a symmetrical 4-DAC architecture (one
DAC dedicated to the positive half and one DAC dedicated to the negative half of the
signal, per channel).

**Objective of the pipeline**: to objectively measure (rather than by ear) the
L/R imbalance of the portable music player, **frequency by frequency**, while eliminating
any possible confusion caused by the inherent coloration of the device used
for the measurement (sound card / input, etc.).
